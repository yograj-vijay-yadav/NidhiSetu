Guidelines for the Demo Micro Finance Scheme for Scheduled Caste beneficiaries.

Eligibility: Scheduled Caste (SC) beneficiaries with an annual family income not exceeding 3,00,000 rupees. Age between 18 and 55 years at the time of application.

Project financing: The maximum project cost eligible under this scheme is 1,40,000 rupees. Financing is provided at 90 percent of the project cost, subject to a maximum loan amount of 1,40,000 rupees. The beneficiary contributes the remaining margin money.

Terms: Interest rate of 6.5 percent per annum (demo). Repayment tenure up to 3 years including a moratorium of 3 months. During the moratorium, interest accrues on the principal and is payable over the repayment period.

Purpose: The scheme supports small income-generating activities such as petty business, self-employment and micro-enterprise activities. Applications for working capital for these purposes may be considered.

Channelizing partners: Applications are routed through State Channelizing Agencies, Public Sector Banks and Regional Rural Banks.

Documentation: Beneficiary requires proof of identity, proof of address, caste certificate, income certificate, project proposal and bank account details.

Important: Final eligibility and approval is decided by the implementing agency. Figures shown are demo placeholders for the NidhiSetu project and must be verified against the latest official guidelines.
