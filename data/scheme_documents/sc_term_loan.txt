Guidelines for the Demo Term Loan Scheme for Scheduled Caste beneficiaries.

Eligibility: Scheduled Caste (SC) beneficiaries aged 18 to 55 years. No upper income cap is configured in the demo dataset.

Project financing: Maximum project cost of 50,00,000 rupees. Financing at 90 percent of project cost subject to a maximum loan amount of 45,00,000 rupees. Beneficiary margin contribution applies.

Terms: Interest rate of 8 percent per annum (demo). Repayment tenure up to 7 years including a moratorium of 6 months. Interest accrues during the moratorium and is recovered over the repayment period.

Purpose: Term loans for larger business ventures, expansion of existing enterprises and self-employment projects.

Channelizing partners: State Channelizing Agencies, Public Sector Banks, Regional Rural Banks and NBFC-MFIs.

Documentation: Identity proof, address proof, caste certificate, income certificate, detailed project report, quotations, and bank account details.

Important: Final eligibility and approval is decided by the implementing agency. Figures shown are demo placeholders for the NidhiSetu project and must be verified against the latest official guidelines.
