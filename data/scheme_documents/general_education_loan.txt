Guidelines for the Demo Concessional Education Loan Scheme.

Eligibility: Open to SC, ST, OBC, Minority, Women and General category students aged 16 to 35 years. Annual family income not exceeding 6,00,000 rupees (demo).

Course financing: Maximum education cost of 20,00,000 rupees for domestic courses. Financing at 90 percent of education cost subject to a maximum loan amount of 18,00,000 rupees. Margin contribution applies beyond defined thresholds.

Terms: Interest rate of 6.5 percent per annum (demo). Repayment tenure up to 10 years. Moratorium of 12 months covering course duration plus a settling period; interest accrues during the moratorium.

Purpose: Tuition fees, examination fees, hostel charges and education-related expenses for degree, diploma and post-graduate courses.

Channelizing partners: State Channelizing Agencies and Public Sector Banks.

Documentation: Admission letter, fee structure, identity proof, income certificate, category certificate and co-borrower details.

Important: Final eligibility and approval is decided by the implementing institution. Figures shown are demo placeholders for the NidhiSetu project and must be verified against the latest official guidelines.
