Guidelines for the Demo Mahila Samriddhi Scheme for Women Entrepreneurs.

Eligibility: Women entrepreneurs aged 18 to 55 years. No upper income cap is configured in the demo dataset.

Project financing: Maximum project cost of 10,00,000 rupees. Financing at 90 percent of project cost subject to a maximum loan amount of 9,00,000 rupees. Beneficiary margin contribution applies.

Terms: Interest rate of 5 percent per annum (demo). Repayment tenure up to 5 years including a moratorium of 6 months. Interest accrues during the moratorium.

Purpose: Starting or expanding micro and small enterprises led by women, including tailoring units, food processing, handicrafts, retail and services.

Channelizing partners: State Channelizing Agencies, Public Sector Banks and NBFC-MFIs.

Documentation: Identity proof, address proof, project proposal, quotations, income details and bank account details.

Important: Final eligibility and approval is decided by the implementing agency. Figures shown are demo placeholders for the NidhiSetu project and must be verified against the latest official guidelines.
